FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay when I submit the request?⌄

No. Submitting the form is free. Our agent will contact you with the fare and payment options.

How fast will you contact me?⌄

Usually within a few minutes during office hours, and first thing next morning otherwise.

Can I change or cancel my ticket?⌄

Yes, subject to the airline's fare rules. We handle date changes and refunds on your behalf.

Do you book tickets for children?⌄

Yes. Choose the number of children (2–11 years) in the search form and we will quote accordingly.

Can I book a multi-city trip?⌄

Yes. Choose “Multi City” in the search form and add up to 5 flights with different routes and dates. Our agent will quote the full itinerary.
